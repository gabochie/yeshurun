## RECOMMENDATION

**Primary (Fastest Path for Ghana + African Diaspora):** **[Selar](https://selar.co/)**
- Built for African creators, supports mobile money + cards
- Handles digital delivery (PDF/EPUB) automatically
- No need to build full checkout/infrastructure initially
- Great for reaching Ghanaians and the global African diaspora
- Can use your own product link immediately

**Secondary (Own Website):** **Paystack**
- Best for integrating on gabochie.com/studio.gabochie.com
- Clean mobile checkout, trusted in Ghana
- Better margins if you handle everything yourself

**Backup:** **Hubtel** (for customers who prefer mobile money without cards)

**Strategy:**
1. **Start with Selar** for quick GTM (target Ghana + African diaspora) - publish digital product now with minimal setup
2. Offer both "Buy on Amazon/KDP" (easy, no gateway needed) AND "Buy Direct" (better margins via Selar or own site)
3. For direct sales: sell PDF/EPUB for instant delivery
4. Keep eBook at $4.99 direct (can compete with Amazon, better margins after fees)

## DIRECT SALES MODEL
- **Digital (PDF/EPUB)**: Instant delivery via email/download link after payment
- **Paperback**: Can redirect to KDP, or fulfill directly later if bulk
- **Bundle**: eBook + future resources
