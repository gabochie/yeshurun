# LEAD MAGNET STRATEGY - "THE UPRIGHT ONE" PREVIEW

## DO WE NEED A SNIPPET?

**YES - Absolutely Required.**

For this type of book (teaching/ministry), a lead magnet will be your highest ROI activity. Here's why:
- Converts cold TikTok traffic (who discover you) into warm leads you can market to
- Builds an email list you OWN (algorithms change, email doesn't)
- Gives value before asking for sale (builds trust with ministry audience)
- Can be used for 2-3 years across all launches

## WHAT TO GIVE

**Recommended:** "From Yaakov to Yeshurun: A Free Preview" (12-15 pages)

Extract from the manuscript:
- Cover page with branding
- Introduction (1-2 pages) 
- Chapter 1: The Symbolism of Yeshurun (full chapter) - This is compelling and shows the core message
- Quick "Which One Are You?" reflection (1 page) - engagement booster
- Call-to-action page (buy full book with direct link)

**Why this content:** Chapter 1 perfectly explains the transformation concept that people will see in your TikTok hooks. They'll want to read more.

## FORMATS

| Format | Priority | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile-Optimized PDF | P0 | Must read well on phones (most traffic will be mobile) - use single column, readable font |
| Web-Friendly Version | P1 | Can embed on landing page for instant access |
| EPUB | P2 | Nice to have but less critical for lead capture |

## DELIVERY MECHANISM

1. User clicks link on TikTok bio/WhatsApp
2. Lands on mobile-first page: "Get the Free Preview"
3. Enters email (or phone number - more common in Ghana)
4. Gets instant download/email with PDF
5. Added to email list with automated follow-up sequence

## FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE (Automated)
Send over 7-10 days:
- Email 1 (Immediate): Deliver PDF + welcome
- Email 2 (Day 2): "Are you a Yaakov, Israel, or Yeshurun?" - engagement
- Email 3 (Day 4): Story/testimonial style - transformation message
- Email 4 (Day 7): "Ready to go deeper?" - link to buy book
- Email 5 (Day 10): Soft reminder + social proof
