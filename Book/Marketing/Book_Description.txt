SHORT DESCRIPTION (Amazon - 150-200 characters)

LONG DESCRIPTION (Amazon - 4000 max, use this version)
-------------------------------------------------------
From the pages of Scripture comes a name of profound transformation: Yeshurun—"The Upright One," "The Beloved."

Appearing only four times in the Hebrew Bible, this poetic name of endearment reveals God's ultimate vision for Israel: not as they are in their struggle, but as they are destined to become. Yet the journey from Jacob to Yeshurun holds a universal message for all who seek to draw closer to the Divine.

In *Yeshurun: The Upright One*, you'll discover:

• **The Threefold Identity** - Explore the profound difference between Yaakov (the struggling heel-catcher), Israel (the wrestler who prevails), and Yeshurun (the upright and beloved one).
• **Ancient Biblical Symbolism** - Uncover the rich imagery of ladders, wrestling matches, tents, and stones that reveal humanity's journey of spiritual transformation.
• **Hebrew Wordplay Revealed** - See how the prophets masterfully used linguistic keys to encode hope for exiles and insight for all generations.
• **Prophetic Hope** - Journey through Isaiah's vision of restoration, where even the lowly "worm Jacob" is destined to become God's beloved Yeshurun.
• **The Inner Path** - Discover a powerful psychological and mystical map for your own spiritual growth—from survival mode, through struggle, to a state of peace and alignment with truth.

Whether you're a student of Scripture, a lover of Hebrew, or someone seeking deeper meaning in your own struggles, *Yeshurun: The Upright One* offers timeless wisdom for the journey from who you are to who you are called to become.

"From Yaakov the struggler to Israel the warrior to Yeshurun the beloved—God is faithful to complete the work He has begun."
