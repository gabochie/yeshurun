## SELAR RECOMMENDATION (Documented)
**Why Selar for Ghana + Global African Diaspora:**
- Mobile money support (MTN Mobile Money, etc.) common in Ghana
- Card payments work for diaspora
- Instant digital delivery (PDF/ePub) out of the box
- No complex payment integration needed
- Can share link directly on WhatsApp/TikTok/IG (mobile-first)
- Handles taxes/VAT better than DIY in many cases
- Faster path to first sale vs building custom checkout

**Recommended Approach:**
1. Launch on Selar first (fastest to market)
2. Also publish on KDP for Amazon discoverability
3. Later add Paystack on own site (gabochie.com/studio.gabochie.com) for better margins
