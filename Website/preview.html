Are You Still In Your Yaakov Season?

The Question We All Ask

Have you ever felt like you're fighting for everything?

Like you're grinding, striving, wrestling with life, people, purpose... yet still feel like you're not quite who you're meant to be?

If yes, then you already understand the story of Jacob.

Meet Yaakov: The One Who Grabs The Heel

Before he became Israel, he was known as Yaakov (יַעֲקֹב).

The name literally means "heel-catcher" or "supplanter".

Jacob came into this world grabbing the heel of his twin brother Esau (Genesis 25:26).

From day one, his life was a fight.

He fought for blessing. He fought for love. He fought to survive.

Sound familiar?

Many of us live our lives as "Yaakov".

• Striving to be accepted

• Fighting for our place

• Working twice as hard to get half as much

• Constantly trying to earn what God already wants to give

The Night Everything Changed

One night, Jacob found himself alone by the Jabbok River (Genesis 32:22-28).

And he wrestled.

Not with a man... but with God.

He wrestled until daybreak. Bruised. Limping. Exhausted.

But he refused to let go until he was blessed.

God spoke these words to him:

"Your name shall no longer be called Yaakov, but Israel; for you have struggled with God and with men, and have prevailed."

— Genesis 32:28 NKJV

From Striver To Prince

Israel (יִשְׂרָאֵל) means, "He who strives with God and prevails."

Notice this: God didn't erase Jacob. He renamed him.

He didn't condemn the wrestler. He crowned him.

The same person that was known for grabbing became known for clinging to God.

The limp remained.

Jacob walked with a limp for the rest of his life (Genesis 32:31-32).

God doesn't always take the struggle away, but He will use it to change your name.

God Has Another Name For You

There's a third name that God uses for His people throughout Scripture: Yeshurun (יְשֻׁרוּן).

It means, "The Upright One".

It's not a name earned by perfection. It's a name spoken in love.

It's the name God calls you when He sees who you're BECOMING, not just who you've been.

Yaakov = Who you were in the fightIsrael = Who you are becoming as you wrestle with GodYeshurun = Who God says you already are in His love

Your Journey Is Theirs

If you're crawling, He's still calling you His.

If you're fighting, He's still wrestling with you to bless you.

If you're limping, He's still leading you.

The journey isn't from failure to perfection. It's from striver to beloved.

God is taking the same people He once called "Jacob" and calling them, "My upright one. My beloved."
