Refund Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026 • Gideon Abochie (gabochie.com)

In short: because the Book is delivered instantly as digital files, sales are generally final once the download is made available — but if anything is faulty, we fix it or refund it. We prefer happy readers over perfect policy language.

1. Digital edition (direct purchase via Selar)

  • Faulty or undelivered: if the file fails to download or open, contact us within 14 days and we will re-deliver or refund in full.
  • Wrong file / duplicate purchase: refunded in full.
  • Change of mind after downloading: no refund, because the digital content was made available to you immediately. You can always read the free preview before buying.
  • EU/UK right of withdrawal: by purchasing you consent to instant access and acknowledge this waives the 14-day cooling-off period (shown at checkout).

2. Kindle & paperback editions (Amazon)

Those orders are handled by Amazon under Amazon's own refund rules: Kindle books can usually be returned within 7 days via "Manage Your Content and Devices"; paperbacks follow Amazon's print return policy.

3. How refunds are paid

Refunds are processed by the original platform (Selar/Amazon) to the original payment method. Mobile money refunds may take 5–10 business days depending on the network.
